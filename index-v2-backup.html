What I found

01

Your Summerlin page is slow to show its first words.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone profile, the last thing to appear on your Summerlin page was the short intro line under the main heading, at 9.3 seconds on one run and 6.1 seconds on a second. First paint came at 5.5 and 3.3 seconds, the speed score was 50 and 43 out of 100, and the page loads about 1.5 MB. Your homepage did better but swung widely: 60 and 70, with its main content (the first slide of the picture slider) showing at 3.2 and 4.7 seconds and the page blocked from responding for 2,660 and 420 milliseconds. These are lab tests, two runs per page; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Summerlin has no semaglutide page of its own.

Your Summerlin page announces “Semaglutide & Tirzepatide Injections Now Available in Summerlin”. There is a Summerlin tirzepatide page, but /summerlin-nv/medical-weight-loss-clinic/semaglutide-injections/ returns a “not found” page and isn't in your site map, and the Summerlin page's semaglutide link goes to the Las Vegas semaglutide page. Henderson and Las Vegas each have both pages. The Las Vegas page does mention the Summerlin clinic, so this may be on purpose.

03

Three Nevada clinics share one phone number, and two share one address.

Your Summerlin, Henderson and Las Vegas pages all list (702) 489-3300. The Henderson and Las Vegas pages both list 9310 S Eastern Ave #122, Las Vegas, NV 89123 and the same Google Maps link in their page code; Summerlin is 10300 W Charleston Blvd #21, Las Vegas, NV 89135. This may be on purpose, such as one front desk for the whole valley. I didn't call the number or check how Google lists each clinic.

04

The homepage description stops in the middle of a sentence.

The description in your homepage code, which Google can show under your name in results, reads “Book a free consultation at our Las Vegas, Costa Mesa, San Marcos, and…” and ends there, so Henderson, Summerlin and Torrance never appear in it. Your Summerlin page's description says “Call today to speak to an experienced provider of weight loss and anti-aging”, without the words semaglutide, tirzepatide or free consultation.

05

The review number on your pages doesn't match what I found for Summerlin.

Your homepage and Summerlin page say “4.9 from 500+ reviews”. The Summerlin listings I found show about 300 to 350: 307 Google reviews on one directory and 348 on another. The 500+ may be a total across all your clinics, but the page doesn't say. The page code on the four pages I checked (homepage, Summerlin, Henderson, Las Vegas) has no review rating markup, which is how search tools read a star rating. I didn't open your Google listing, so I can't say which count is current.

06

Some small text is hard to read.

My accessibility check found tan text (#cfb292) on white at contrast ratios between 1.7 and 2.0 where 4.5 is expected, on the small uppercase labels and on links such as “Hormone optimization” on your Summerlin page. On the homepage, an 11-pixel grey footnote scores 2.35. Accessibility scored 96 out of 100 on both pages, and this was the only automated failure.

07

A few small slips.

The business markup lists Facebook and Instagram but no YouTube channel, and your homepage has no YouTube link, although a TrimBodyMd channel with 66 public videos exists. The location choice in the consultation form lists Henderson, Newport Beach, Torrance, San Marcos and Summerlin, with no Las Vegas or Costa Mesa. 779 of the 790 addresses in your site map carry the same last-modified date, September 19, 2026. I didn't submit the form.

What's working

Your phone number is at the top of every page as a tap-to-call link beside a free-consultation button, and the consultation form is short (first and last name, phone, email, location, and what you need help with). The site has pages for each clinic and for services at each clinic, 790 addresses in the site map, and 789 of them loaded when I checked (one timed out). Lighthouse scored SEO 100 and best practices 100 on both pages I tested.
